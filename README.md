# Glizzy Cart

A one-person, steam-only hot dog trailer based in **Kyle, Texas**.

## Concept

- **Format:** a small enclosed walk-in concession trailer (about 6x10). The operator stands inside and serves through a front window. Style reference: [The Homedog LLC](https://www.youtube.com/@thehomedogllc).
- **Cooking:** steam only, no griddle or fryer. Dogs are held in a hot water/vinegar bath, toppings in electric hot wells, buns in a bun steamer, cold toppings in a refrigerated rail. Caramelized onions are cooked at the commissary.
- **Power:** a lithium battery bank instead of a gas generator, recharged overnight.
- **Selling spots:** home-improvement store lots (Lowe's / Home Depot), Kyle events (Kyle Fair, Pie in the Sky), food parks in Buda and San Marcos.
